## Marissa Chen, 34 — Music Journalist, Pitchfork Contributor

"As someone who writes about music for a living, I'm always skeptical of tools that claim to 'analyze' artistry—too often they reduce complex work to shallow metrics. This app, however, strikes a thoughtful balance. The theme extraction isn't perfect (it occasionally conflates distinct concepts), but the sentiment evolution feature is genuinely insightful for tracking an artist's trajectory across albums. I ran Radiohead through it while working on a retrospective piece and it helped me articulate shifts I'd felt but couldn't quantify. It's a starting point, not a replacement for critical analysis, but a valuable one."

**Rating: 4/5**

## Dmitri Volkov, 29 — Senior Data Scientist

"From a technical standpoint, solid implementation. LDA is a reasonable choice for topic extraction from a corpus this size, though I'd be curious to see how BERTopic or other transformer-based approaches would compare. The async job architecture is smart—you can tell they thought about UX for longer-running analyses. Tested it with several artists and the results were reproducible. The sentiment analysis via TextBlob is admittedly basic (lexicon-based approaches have known limitations), but for a free tool, this delivers. Clean interface, no bloat. Bookmarked."

**Rating: 4/5**
